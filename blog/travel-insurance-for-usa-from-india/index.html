Travel Insurance for USA from India: A Complete Guide

Why Travel Insurance is Essential for USA Travel

The United States has one of the most expensive healthcare systems in the world. A routine visit to an emergency room can cost between USD 2,000 and USD 10,000, while a hospital stay for a serious condition can easily exceed USD 100,000. For Indian travellers, these costs are far beyond what most can afford out of pocket. Travel insurance provides critical financial protection against medical emergencies, trip cancellations, lost baggage, and other unforeseen events during your stay in the USA.

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) regulates travel insurance products sold in India, ensuring transparent policy wordings, standardised terms, and fair claim settlement practices. Policies sold by IRDAI-registered insurers must clearly list inclusions, exclusions, and provide a free-look period of 15 days for cancellations.

For general travel insurance for Indian travellers, the fundamentals of coverage remain the same regardless of destination, but USA-specific policies typically require higher sum insured limits due to the elevated cost of medical care.

USA Visa Types and Insurance Requirements

Insurance requirements for USA travel depend on your visa category. While tourist visa holders are not legally required to have coverage, several other visa types mandate health insurance as a condition of stay. Understanding these requirements is essential when planning your trip.

B1/B2 Tourist Visa Insurance

The B1/B2 visa is the most common visa for Indian travellers visiting the USA for business or tourism. The US government does not mandate travel insurance for B1/B2 visa holders. The DS-160 visa application form does not ask for insurance details, and visa officers typically do not request proof of coverage at the time of interview. However, travel insurance is strongly recommended given the high cost of healthcare in the USA. Many Indian travellers opt for policies with medical coverage of at least USD 100,000 to USD 250,000. Some travellers choose even higher limits of USD 500,000 or USD 1,000,000 for greater peace of mind.

F1 Student Health Insurance

Indian students holding F1 visas are generally required by their universities to maintain health insurance throughout their studies. Most US universities automatically enrol international students in their institutional health plan, which covers on-campus health services, doctor visits, hospitalisation, prescription drugs, mental health services, and emergency care. The annual premium for university-sponsored plans ranges from USD 1,500 to USD 4,000 depending on the institution and coverage level. Some universities allow students to waive the institutional plan if they provide proof of comparable coverage from a private insurer. Indian students should check their university's specific requirements before departure and consider buying a student travel insurance plan that meets the institution's minimum standards.

J1 Exchange Visitor Insurance

J1 exchange visitors are required by US federal regulation to maintain medical insurance that meets specific minimum standards throughout their programme. The US Department of State mandates that J1 insurance policies provide: medical benefits of at least USD 100,000 per accident or illness, deductible not exceeding USD 500 per accident or illness, repatriation of remains coverage of at least USD 25,000, and medical evacuation coverage of at least USD 50,000. The policy must be underwritten by an insurance carrier with an A.M. Best rating of A- or higher or equivalent rating from another recognised agency. Sponsoring organisations are responsible for verifying that their J1 participants maintain compliant coverage, and failure to do so can result in termination of the programme.

For those also travelling to Europe, a Schengen travel insurance policy has different minimum requirements, including EUR 30,000 medical cover and validity across all Schengen member states.

H1B/H4 Travel Insurance

H1B work visa holders and their H4 dependents are typically covered by employer-sponsored health insurance plans once they begin working in the USA. However, during the transition period between arriving in the USA and the start of employment, there is often a coverage gap. Short-term travel insurance or visitor health insurance can bridge this gap, providing medical coverage for the initial weeks. Additionally, when H1B or H4 visa holders travel back to India and return to the USA, travel insurance can cover trip-related risks such as flight delays, lost baggage, and medical emergencies during transit.

Key Coverage to Look For

When selecting a travel insurance policy for the USA, focus on the following core coverage areas. Understanding these will help you compare policies effectively and avoid gaps in protection.

It is also useful to review common exclusions in travel insurance to understand what is not covered before you purchase a policy.

Medical Emergency Cover

Medical cover is the most important component of USA travel insurance. Look for a policy with a minimum sum insured of USD 100,000, though USD 250,000 to USD 500,000 is preferable. The policy should cover hospitalisation, outpatient treatment, emergency dental care, prescription medicines, diagnostic tests, and ambulance services. Check whether the insurer has a network of cashless hospitals in the USA, as this can significantly reduce out-of-pocket expenses. Also verify whether pre-existing medical conditions are covered — many policies exclude them unless a specific add-on is purchased.

Emergency Evacuation and Repatriation

Emergency medical evacuation covers transportation to the nearest appropriate medical facility in the event of a serious illness or injury. Repatriation of remains covers the cost of returning your body to India in the unfortunate event of death. These are critical coverages that can cost tens of thousands of dollars without insurance. Most comprehensive USA travel insurance policies include evacuation cover of at least USD 50,000 to USD 500,000.

Baggage and Personal Belongings

Baggage loss, theft, or damage cover reimburses you for lost or damaged luggage and personal items. Delayed baggage benefit provides a fixed daily amount to purchase essential items if your bags are delayed beyond 6 to 12 hours. Baggage cover limits for USA policies typically range from USD 500 to USD 2,000. Keep receipts for valuable items and declare them when purchasing the policy if they exceed the per-item limit.

Trip Cancellation and Interruption

Trip cancellation cover reimburses non-refundable trip expenses including flights, hotels, and tours if you must cancel due to a covered reason such as illness, accident, death of a family member, or natural disaster. Trip interruption cover pays for the unused portion of your trip if you need to return early. Given the high cost of USA travel, having adequate cancellation cover is important. Purchase your policy immediately after booking your trip to maximise the cancellation coverage period.

Cost of Travel Insurance for USA

The premium for USA travel insurance depends on several factors. The sum insured is the primary driver — policies with USD 500,000 medical cover cost significantly more than those with USD 100,000. Age is another major factor; travellers over 60 can expect premiums two to three times higher than younger travellers. Trip duration also affects cost, with each additional week adding to the premium. Optional add-ons such as pre-existing disease cover, adventure sports cover, and cancel-for-any-reason cover increase the premium further. For a typical two-week trip, a traveller under 50 can expect to pay between Rs. 2,000 and Rs. 8,000 for a good quality policy with adequate limits.

How to Choose the Right Policy

Choosing the right travel insurance policy for the USA requires careful evaluation. Start by determining the minimum coverage you need based on your visa type and trip duration. Compare at least three policies from IRDAI-registered insurers, focusing on the sum insured, coverage inclusions, sub-limits, deductibles, and exclusions. Verify the insurer's claim settlement ratio and check their network of cashless hospitals in the USA. Read the policy document thoroughly, especially the fine print regarding pre-existing conditions, deductible amounts, and claim procedures. Purchase the policy immediately after booking your flight to benefit from trip cancellation cover. Keep a copy of the policy and the 24/7 emergency assistance number accessible throughout your trip. If you are unsure about any aspect of the policy, contact the insurer's customer service for clarification before purchasing.

Conclusion

Travel insurance for the USA from India is not just about meeting visa requirements — it is about protecting yourself from the potentially devastating financial impact of medical emergencies and travel disruptions in one of the most expensive healthcare environments in the world. Whether you are travelling on a B1/B2 tourist visa, studying on an F1 visa, participating in a J1 exchange programme, or working on an H1B visa, having appropriate insurance coverage provides peace of mind and financial security. Compare policies carefully, understand the coverage and exclusions, and purchase from an IRDAI-registered insurer for a reliable experience.
